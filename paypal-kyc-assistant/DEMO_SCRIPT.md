# PayReady India demo script

1. Open the dashboard and switch between Personal and Business preparation flows.
2. Complete several checklist items and show the readiness score plus next action.
3. Ask why verification may be delayed and open the official source link.
4. Load a safe synthetic document sample, lower readability, and review the result.
5. Show that a PAN-like value is rejected rather than processed.
6. If Sandbox credentials are configured, create the USD 1.00 test order and point
   out the Sandbox/KYC separation notice.
7. Stop the backend, refresh to demonstrate the friendly error, restart it, and retry.

Close with: “PayReady is an independent preparation prototype. It does not access a
PayPal account, store identity documents, or make a KYC decision.”
